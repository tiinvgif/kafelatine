KAFÉLATINE

Nestled on a quiet corner of the street, the
KAFÉLATINE is a cozy haven of warmth and
character. Soft golden light shines through
the windows, putting a warm glow on the
weathered wooden tables and warm seats
inside. The air is filled with the rich perfume
of freshly made coffee, which mixes with the
scent of warm pastries that line the counter.

KAFÉLATINE is located at Zone 6 Pob.,
Palanginan, Iba, Zambales.
